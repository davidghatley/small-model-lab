FAQ

+ What sweetener do you use?

Organic cane sugar only. No high-fructose corn syrup, no sucralose.

+ Is the bottling line deterministic?

Close. Fill volume holds within +/- 2 ml across 50,000 bottles per run.

+ Can I get batch data?

Yes. Each cap carries a code that maps to the lab sheet for that batch.

+ Do you ship outside the US?

Canada and the UK, in 24-bottle cases.
